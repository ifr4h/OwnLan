## Research discipline

1.  Prefer primary sources.
2.  Re-check competitor pricing/features.
3.  Date-stamp volatile facts.
4.  Distinguish vendor claims from independent facts.
5.  Label calculations as estimates.
6.  Label projections as scenarios.
7.  Never fabricate traction.
