# Master Feature Roadmap

Priority: **P0 essential**, **P1 important**, **P2 later value**, **P3
experimental/evidence required**.

## Phase 0 --- Validation

P0: - 20--30 instructor interviews - observe real admin workflows -
competitor teardown - pricing/freemium testing - current-tool mapping -
payments research - trademark/name clearance - design-partner cohort

P1: - landing page/waitlist - learner interviews

## Phase 1 --- MVP: Run My Business

### Business

P0 auth, organisation, instructor profile, hours, durations, pricing.\
P1 service areas, vehicle, cancellation policy, time off.

### Pupils

P0 CRUD/archive, contacts, pickup, notes, lessons, balances, packages,
import.\
P1 test date, guardian/emergency details.

### Diary

P0 day/week, bookings, recurrence, availability, travel time, conflict
warnings.\
P1 test blocks, sync, slot calculation.

### Daily workflow

P0 today, next pupil, navigation, complete lesson, payment status, next
booking, notes, progress.\
P1 voice notes.

### Teaching

P0 syllabus, progress, notes.\
P1 summaries, next focus, mock tests.

### Money

P0 payments, balances, packages, revenue, outstanding, expenses.\
P1 receipts, profit overview, exports.

### Learner

P0 portal, next lesson, history, progress, balance.\
P1 next focus.

## Phase 2 --- Product-Market Fit / Automation

### Payments

P0 payment links, cards, reconciliation, packages, receipts, reminders.\
P1 deposits, refunds, cancellation charges, Pay by Bank/Open Banking,
low-balance reminders.

### Scheduling Intelligence V1

P0 geography-aware availability, gap detection, travel suggestions,
intelligent waiting list, cancellation matching, slot offers.\
P1 auto-book after acceptance.

### CRM

P0 enquiries, waiting list, lead→pupil.\
P1 pipeline, follow-ups, automated responses.\
P2 reactivation/lost-lead analytics.

### Storefront

P0 public profile, booking page, pricing, availability, service areas,
enquiry, booking.\
P1 reviews/branding.\
P2 custom domain.

## Phase 3 --- Growth Engine

### Revenue intelligence

P0 targets, forecasts, lost-revenue detection, empty-slot value,
utilisation, travel efficiency.\
P1 revenue/hour, cancellation loss, demand heatmaps.\
P2 pricing suggestions.

### Scheduling Optimiser V2

Optimise using geography, travel, working hours, pupil availability,
duration, tests, preferences, waiting list and revenue opportunity.

### AI teaching/admin

P0 speech→notes, structured progress.\
P1 learner summaries, next-focus suggestions, pattern/weakness
detection.\
P2 private-practice suggestions.

### Private practice

P1 goals, learner logs, confidence reflection, history, instructor
visibility.\
P2 practice routes.

### Finance V2

P0 accountant export, receipt OCR.\
P1 categorisation, bank feeds, matching, tax estimate, tax-year
summary.\
P2 accounting integrations/MTD workflow.\
P3 direct HMRC.

## Phase 4 --- OwnLane Network

### Marketplace

P0 postcode search, real availability, filters, profiles, reviews,
enquiry, booking, intelligent matching.\
P1 preference matching/ranking.

### Instructor network

P0 learner referral, overflow, network waiting list, referral tracking.\
P1 holiday cover, capacity sharing, local networks, school overflow.

### Driving Passport

P0 portable progress, verified history, skill history, consented
transfer/handover.\
P1 private practice, mocks, journey timeline.

### Family Mode

P1 guardian access, payments, balance, consented progress, practice
goals, receipts.\
P2 notifications.

### Native apps

Instructor iOS/Android and Learner iOS/Android.

## Phase 5 --- Industry Infrastructure

### Business Twin

Combines diary, pupils, money, demand, travel, progress, leads, payments
and history to proactively surface risks/opportunities.

### Autopilot V3

Policy-constrained automation around revenue targets, maximum travel,
working boundaries, pupil priority, cancellations and geography.

### Driving Graph

Network intelligence across instructors, learners, geography, lessons,
skills, availability, demand, referrals and test centres.

### Partner ecosystem

P1 insurance, leasing, dual controls, breakdown, servicing/MOT,
fuel/charging.\
P2 tyres, accountants, CPD/training, vehicle marketplace.

## Evidence-required ideas

-   reputation score: fairness/transparency required
-   dynamic pricing: instructor-controlled; avoid exploitative design
-   waiting-list transfer: consent-based referrals, never sale of
    personal data
-   direct HMRC: only after demand/compliance work
-   test cancellation scraping: do not build
