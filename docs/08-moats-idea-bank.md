# Competitive Moats & Idea Bank

## Moat test

If a competitor can copy it in six months, it may be useful but is not
strong defensibility.

If it becomes more valuable as more instructors/learners use OwnLane, it
may compound.

## Table stakes

Competitors already provide combinations of diaries, gaps, pupil/parent
apps, progress, finances, reminders, enquiries, booking, payments,
teaching aids and logs.

Execute these well; do not call them the moat.

## OwnLane Autopilot

Instructor defines working/business rules; OwnLane handles or proposes
cancellations, payments, gaps, rebooking, route and revenue actions
within those boundaries.

## Empty Seat Engine

Cancellation → eligible learners → availability/geography/travel ranking
→ offer → acceptance → payment → calendars update.

## Intelligent Scheduling

Understand mobile geography, traffic/travel, pickups, lesson duration,
tests, learner availability, instructor preferences and economics.
Eventually suggest schedule changes that save travel/create sellable
capacity.

## Business Twin

Live model that predicts risks/opportunities instead of only showing
historical dashboards.

## Revenue Optimisation

Targets, empty capacity, outstanding money, travel inefficiency,
utilisation, learners without future bookings and capacity forecasts.

## Intelligent Matching

Match learners and instructors using actual availability, geography,
preferences, price, transmission, capacity and teaching fit---not merely
postcode directories.

## Driving Passport

Learner-controlled portable progress/history, skills, mocks, goals and
private practice, transferable with consent.

## Private Practice

Instructor goals + learner/family practice logs + reflection +
instructor visibility.

## Teaching Intelligence

Parked voice input → structured notes/progress/next focus/learner
summary; later pattern detection.

## Confidence Tracking

Learner reflection + instructor assessment; surface patterns without
pretending to diagnose psychology.

## OwnLane Network

Full instructor → consented referral to another OwnLane instructor. Add
overflow, holiday cover, capacity sharing and local networks.

## Family Mode

Consent-based guardian payments, balances, progress and private-practice
support.

## Partner ecosystem

Insurance, leasing, dual controls, breakdown, servicing, fuel/charging,
training, accountants, vehicle marketplace.

## Driving Graph

Long-term network intelligence across instructors, learners, geography,
lessons, skills, availability, demand, referrals and test centres.

## Reputation

Potential verified operational signals beyond reviews; must be fair,
transparent and contestable.

## Dynamic Pricing Assistant

Instructor-controlled suggestions only; avoid exploitative/opaque
pricing.

## Moat stack

1.  workflow depth
2.  connected operational data
3.  scheduling intelligence
4.  learner network
5.  instructor network
6.  marketplace liquidity
7.  distribution
8.  proprietary matching/optimisation history
