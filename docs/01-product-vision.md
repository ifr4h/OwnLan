# Product Vision

## Problem

Independent UK driving instructors run mobile small businesses but often
stitch together calendars, messaging, bank transfers, spreadsheets,
notes, progress records, receipts, accounting tools, websites and lead
sources.

Existing competitors already provide many basic management functions.
OwnLane cannot win merely by having a diary, pupil records, payments and
progress tracking.

## Vision

Give independent instructors the infrastructure advantages of a large
driving-school franchise while allowing them to:

-   remain independent
-   own customer relationships
-   set prices
-   set working hours
-   retain their brand
-   control how their business operates

Long term:

> Independent instructors should collectively have more useful
> infrastructure through OwnLane than a national franchise can provide
> individually.

## Product surfaces

### Instructor responsive web

Diary, pupils, CRM, finance, reports, settings, pricing, availability
and administration.

### Instructor native app --- later

Daily cockpit: today, next pupil, pickup, payment status, complete
lesson, progress, next booking, notes, earnings. Design for safe parked
use.

### Learner portal --- MVP

Next lesson, history, progress, balance/package and next focus.

### Learner native app --- later

Booking, payments, reminders, progress, private practice, Driving
Passport and marketplace.

### Public marketplace --- later

SEO profiles/location pages, postcode search, real availability,
filters, matching and booking.

### Internal admin --- later

Support and operational tools.

## Core principle

> An instructor should never have to enter the same piece of information
> twice.

Booking once should update diary, learner view, availability, projected
revenue and reminders.

Completing a lesson once should update lesson state, balance/payment,
progress, notes, learner summary and financial records as appropriate.

## Product levels

### Level 1 --- Run my business

Diary, pupils, progress, balances, payments, reminders, finance.

### Level 2 --- Grow and optimise

Scheduling optimisation, CRM, revenue intelligence, marketplace,
matching and automation.

### Level 3 --- OwnLane Network

Driving Passport, referrals, overflow capacity, network availability,
private practice, partner services and financial infrastructure.

## Category

Simple: **digital operating platform for independent driving
instructors.**

Ambitious: **operating network for learning to drive.**
