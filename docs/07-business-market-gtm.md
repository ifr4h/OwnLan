# Business Model, Market & GTM

## Thesis

Independent driving instructors are fragmented mobile businesses with
recurring scheduling, payment, pupil, teaching and finance workflows.

Existing software validates demand, but subscription-only economics can
cap the opportunity. OwnLane's larger ambition comes from infrastructure
across both instructor and learner activity.

## Potential model

### Free/generous core

Diary, pupil records, basic progress, learner portal, basic payment
records and eventually marketplace supply.

### Pro

Indicative concept: \~£19.99--£24.99/month, not final. Advanced
automation, finance, analytics, CRM/growth, scheduling and branding.

### School/business

Future multi-instructor tier, pricing to validate.

### Additional future revenue

-   marketplace/service fees
-   compliant payment economics
-   growth tools
-   school plans
-   partner/referral commissions

## Flywheel

Instructor joins → brings learners → learners use OwnLane → learner
demand grows → marketplace becomes valuable → instructors gain customers
→ more instructors join.

## Metrics

Instructor: - registered/activated/weekly active - teaching-day usage -
pupils/lessons managed - retention/churn - free→paid

Learner: - activation - booking/payment usage - referrals - marketplace
usage

Financial: - MRR/ARR - ARPU - CAC/LTV - gross margin - GMV -
monetisation rate - payment cost

Network: - liquidity - match rate - time to match - referrals - demand
retained in network

## GTM

1.  design partners
2.  dense local cohort
3.  second/third geographic cluster
4.  referrals
5.  PDI acquisition
6.  learner distribution
7.  marketplace

## Founding instructor programme

Potentially free Pro/direct founder access/migration help in exchange
for real usage and structured feedback.

## Fundraising

Bootstrap validation/MVP if possible. Raise only when evidence supports
it: active instructors, retention, lessons, GMV, acquisition and clear
monetisation/network signals.

Never fabricate traction.
