# Brand & Company Decisions

## Working brand

**OwnLane**

Preferred: - Brand: OwnLane - Prospective legal company: OwnLane Ltd -
Trademark to investigate: word mark OWNLANE

Avoid narrowing the company to "OwnLane Software Ltd" because the vision
extends beyond SaaS.

## Tagline

**Run your driving school. Keep your independence.**

Alternative:

**Your pupils. Your business. Your lane.**

## Mission

**Give every driving instructor the power to build their own driving
school.**

## Category

**OwnLane is building the operating network for independent driving
instruction.**

## Personality

Capable, calm, independent, human, modern, practical.

## Tone

Plain English, confident without hype, useful before clever, financially
transparent, respectful of instructors as business owners.

Avoid gig-economy language, patronising digital-literacy language,
employee-like framing, surveillance-style HMRC language and exaggerated
AI claims.

## Trademark/company status

Working brand; this document is not legal clearance.

Before launch: - UKIPO exact/similar search - correct
classes/specification - Companies House check - domain(s) - professional
IP advice if appropriate - word-mark filing if appropriate

Company registration and trademark registration are separate.

## Domain

`theownlane.com` has been observed as taken.

Do not distort the brand solely for an exact .com. Investigate options
such as ownlane.co.uk, ownlane.uk, ownlane.app or getownlane.com;
availability must be verified directly.

## Company formation

Likely eventual structure: private company limited by shares, simple
initial share structure unless professional advice suggests otherwise.

If address privacy matters, arrange a legitimate
registered-office/director service address before incorporation.

Reasonable sequence: validation/prototype → commitment → incorporation
before serious trading/contracts/public launch → company owns relevant
IP/contracts/assets.

No need to become a sole trader merely to prototype.
