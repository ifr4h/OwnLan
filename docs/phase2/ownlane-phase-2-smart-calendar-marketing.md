# OwnLane Phase 2 — Smart Calendar Marketing Notes

## More to come

The OwnLane Diary is only the start.

Phase 2 will make the calendar more aware of the things happening around every lesson, using information OwnLane already knows from the instructor's diary, pupils, payments and bookings.

The important part: instructors should not have to maintain extra admin for this to work.

---

## Short website version

### A diary that starts thinking ahead

We're working on a smarter OwnLane calendar that can spot useful gaps, understand travel between lessons, remember how pupils usually book and help instructors make better use of their day.

OwnLane will be able to surface things such as:

- pupils who could fit into a cancellation
- tight travel between lessons
- pupils with no next lesson booked
- remaining lesson credit
- upcoming tests and the lessons already booked before them
- useful booking patterns
- suggested times when booking the same pupil again

All from information that's already in OwnLane.

No extra spreadsheet. No extra admin.

---

## More to come — compact version

### Smart Calendar

OwnLane's calendar is becoming more than a diary.

It will be able to spot useful gaps, match suitable pupils, understand travel between lessons, recognise regular booking patterns and make rebooking much faster.

If a cancellation opens two hours on Thursday, OwnLane could show which pupils actually fit there and why.

If Jake normally books Sunday mornings, his usual slot can be ready when you tap Book next.

If a pupil's lesson credit is running low or their test is approaching, the relevant context can appear where you need it.

The aim is simple: make the diary more useful without giving instructors more admin.

---

## Feature-page version

### The calendar should know more than what's booked

A normal diary knows that Jake is booked at 10am.

OwnLane can already see much more around that lesson.

It knows what comes before and after it. It can understand lesson duration, pickup locations, travel time, future bookings, test dates and lesson credit.

Phase 2 will start connecting those pieces.

That means OwnLane could notice:

**A useful gap**

```text
2h free on Thursday
3 pupils could fit here
```

**A tight journey**

```text
12 min until your next lesson
Estimated journey: 17 min
```

**A pupil with nothing booked next**

```text
Sarah usually has a lesson every week.
Nothing is booked after Friday.
```

**A block nearing its end**

```text
2h left after today's lesson
```

**A cancellation that can be recovered**

```text
2h opened Thursday at 14:00
3 waiting pupils could fit
```

**A better Book next experience**

```text
Sunday 13 September
10:00–11:00
Usual pickup

[Book]
```

The instructor should not have to configure all of this manually.

OwnLane learns from the way the business is already being run.

## Marketing principles

Do not market this as:

- AI scheduling
- automatic optimisation
- a productivity score
- a promise to fill every gap
- a system that predicts pupil readiness
- a system that automatically rearranges lessons

The better message is:

> OwnLane notices useful things from information that's already there.

Use real situations in marketing.

Prefer:

```text
2h free on Thursday
3 pupils could fit
```

over:

```text
Optimise your scheduling with intelligent recommendations
```

Prefer:

```text
Sarah usually books Tuesdays.
Nothing is booked next week.
```

over:

```text
Never lose track of your pupils
```

Prefer:

```text
12 min between lessons
Estimated drive: 17 min
```

over:

```text
Smarter travel management
```

The data should demonstrate the feature.

## Recommended "More to come" final copy

### A smarter calendar is coming

We're building more intelligence into the OwnLane Diary without adding more admin.

It will be able to notice useful gaps, show which pupils could fit, flag tight travel between lessons, recognise regular booking patterns and make booking the next lesson much faster.

It can also connect things that normally live in separate places, such as lesson credit, future bookings and upcoming tests.

So if two hours suddenly opens on Thursday, OwnLane won't just show an empty space. It can help you understand who could realistically use it.

And when a regular pupil needs another lesson, OwnLane can already have the likely time, duration and pickup ready.

The calendar should remember the details so the instructor doesn't have to.
